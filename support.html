Get in touch

Email cytoswarm@pacificfrontdesk.com. One person reads it, so a reply usually takes a day or two rather than an hour.

It helps to include your device, your iOS version, and - if it is about a particular run - the daily number shown on the home screen.

Common questions

How do I play?

Drag anywhere to move; a joystick appears under your thumb. Your weapons fire on their own at whatever is closest. Enemies drop gems, gems fill the bar, and a full bar pauses the fight so you can take one of three upgrades.

Why can I not see other people on the daily board?

The online board needs the device to be signed in to iCloud. Without it the game still keeps your own best time for the day, and the board shows that instead of everyone.

I bought something and did not get it

Open the Store from the home screen and tap Restore purchases. If it still does not appear, email us with the date of the purchase and we will sort it out.

My progress disappeared

Progress is stored on the device. Deleting the app deletes it with the app, and it does not currently sync between an iPhone and an iPad. Cloud save is on the list.

Something in the game says something odd

The one-liners enemies say are updated from a published file rather than shipped in the app, so they change without an update. If one of them is wrong, offensive or just bad, email us and it can be pulled the same day - it does not need an App Store review.
