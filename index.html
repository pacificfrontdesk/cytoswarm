Cytoswarm

Survive ten minutes in the dish.

You are one cell. Everything else in the dish wants in. You never fire a shot on purpose - your weapons do that for you - so the whole game is where you stand, what you pick up, and which three upgrades you take when the fight pauses.

Coming to the App Store

What is in it

  • Ten-minute runs. The clock counts down; reach 0:00 and you have cleared it.
  • Seventeen kinds of enemy, each with a habit to answer: chargers that dash, sappers that mine the ground ahead of you, warpers that blink past your kiting, shielders you have to get around.
  • Twenty-one upgrades, four weapon evolutions, and three cards on every level-up.
  • A daily run on the same seed for everyone, with a board.
  • Ten looks for the same game - microlife, spores, atoms, aliens, neon, gods - each with its own palette, shapes and floor.
