Privacy policy

Cytoswarm - Pacific Front Desk LLC

Short version: the game keeps your progress on your device. The only thing it ever sends anywhere is a daily score, and only if you play the daily run.

What stays on your device

Your coins, unlocked characters, training, skins and themes, your run history, your settings, and your records. This lives in the app's own storage on the device. It is not sent to us, and deleting the app deletes it.

What leaves your device

Daily leaderboard. If you play the daily run, the game submits that run to Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) so it can appear on the day's board: a display name, the time you survived, your level, your kills, and which character you used. The display name is generated for you (for example "Survivor-816") and you can change it on the board screen. Entries are tied to an identifier Apple derives from your iCloud account; we never receive your Apple Account, your email or your real name. If the device is not signed in to iCloud, nothing is submitted and the board shows only your own runs.

Cloud save. Your coins, unlocks, training and records are mirrored to your own private iCloud database so a new device picks them up. It is held in your Apple Account, not on our servers - we cannot read it, and deleting the app or turning off iCloud for it removes our access to it entirely. Nothing about it is shared with anyone.

Bark lines. The game downloads a small file of one-liners from pacificfrontdesk.github.io so they can be refreshed without an app update. It is an ordinary web request and sends nothing about you beyond what any web request includes.

Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We are told what you own so the app can unlock it; we never see your payment details.

Advertising

The current version has no advertising SDK and no ad tracking. The "watch an ad" options are simulated placeholders. Before any real ad network ships, this page will be updated to say exactly what it collects, and the App Store privacy labels will be updated with it.

Analytics

There is no analytics SDK and no third-party tracker in the app. We do not sell data, and there is nothing here to sell.

Children

The game is suitable for a general audience and does not ask for personal information. The only field you can type into is the leaderboard display name - please do not put a real name in it.

Deleting what we hold

To have your leaderboard entries removed, email cytoswarm@pacificfrontdesk.com from the device in question or tell us the display name you used. Deleting the app removes everything held locally.

Changes

If what the app collects changes, this page changes before that version ships, and the date at the bottom moves.
